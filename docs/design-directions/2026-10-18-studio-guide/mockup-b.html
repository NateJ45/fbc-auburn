How a page is built

A page is a stack of sections, one on top of the other. You edit what is inside a section. You do not have to think about colors or spacing: the design decides those from where the section sits on the page.

Seeing it as you type

  1. 1
    Click Live preview in the bar at the top of the Studio.
  2. 2
    Pick the page on the left. The real page appears beside it.
  3. 3
    Click any words on the page to jump to the box that holds them.
  4. 4
    Type. The page updates as you go.
  5. 5
    Click Publish when it reads right.

The preview shows your draft.

What you see in Live preview is your unpublished draft, not the live site. That is the point: you see the change before anyone else does.

Nothing is live until you press Publish.

If you make a mess and have not published, close the tab and walk away.

Never click Remove field.

It deletes that box's words on every page. Ask Nathan first.
