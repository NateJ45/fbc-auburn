## The panel

Three reviewers worked from the contact sheets and the live tool on 2026-10-27: a church graphic
designer (hierarchy, type, brand, print), a pastor of a small country church plus a strict AI-slop
auditor (would members and neighbors trust it and read it; every template tell), and a UX designer
who drove the Studio tool at 375 and 1280 through the kit harness.

## Decision

2026-10-27 (PROVISIONAL: made overnight from the panel while Nathan was away; Nathan confirms, or drops a look, when he reviews): keep **two looks** and let the pastor choose per graphic. **Light** (`porch`, the
default) and **Bold** (`press`). The panel split on purpose: one look is right for print and
the website's family, the other for the feed and a door. Night is retired. Fixes taken into both:
titles never fitted below 8% of the short side on social sizes, minimum text sizes (26px at 1080,
about 11pt in print), the times at least as large as the body, the church's name and web address on
every shared size, the yard sign cut to the name, the title and one address, a hanger hole on the
door hanger, and every slop tell removed from Bold (no speckle over type or photos, no torn edges,
no sticker tags, photos warm and natural, verse without a photo). Light's photo slab is the red
wall, per DESIGN.md.
