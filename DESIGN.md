## Overview

Status 2026-10-17: direction picked, A blended with B and C (`docs/design-directions/2026-10-17-fbc-auburn/`,
`## Decision`). Archetype: image-led, but airy and white, with the stone art carrying the page until
the photo session. Home opens with the masthead ("First Baptist Church of" over a huge "Auburn") beside an arched photo; inner pages lead with a big stone icon and a plain navy Young Serif H1
beside an arched photo. Density airy. Imagery: arched photos (`ArchHero.astro`, the
text-and-picture band); with no photo yet the arch holds the brick-cross mark or a soft navy tint, never
a grey box. In the code: `src/styles/auburn.css` (the brand layer), `StoneHeading.astro`,
`ArchHero.astro`; the three stepping stones are the section divider on inner pages.

Update 2026-10-21, the brick brand: the pebble art is gone from the site. The section marker is
three stepped bricks from the mark's corner; the hero path is a low brick wall of three courses
stepping up to the This Sunday card; the card, the sermon art and the quiet panels sit on
running-bond tiles; the walk's numbered stones are numbered bricks. Generated by
`npm run gen:stones` from `scripts/lib/brick-courses.mjs` (decision:
`docs/design-directions/2026-10-21-brick-path/`). The letterforms followed the same evening
(see "Brick letterforms" under Typography): the masthead "Auburn" and the page H1s (the footer signature was retired 2026-10-24)
were brick-filled letters, and the 404 headline and the sermon art with no picture were cut out of a wall. All of them went back to plain Young Serif on 2026-10-24 (too busy).

Update 2026-10-17, the bold home pass: Home carries a full-height ribbon of stones in the four
mark colours (`StoneRibbon.astro`, `src/scripts/ribbon.ts`) on a plain white page with no tinted
bands or dividers, a photo hero on Home with the welcome set over it in white (2026-10-23), and photos (stock stand-ins until the photo
session). Decision: `docs/design-directions/2026-10-17-fbc-auburn/README.md`.

Update 2026-10-25, the restraint pass (site audit, graphic design and UX reviews). The motif, the red and
Young Serif were each used at every opportunity, so none of them was special. Now:

- **One red button per page**: the page's single primary next step (SectionRenderer gives the red to the first
  block that asks for it). Every other button is solid navy or the navy outline. Card meta lines, menu arrows,
  the walk's numbered bricks and every connector are navy; auburn is the mark and that one button.
- **Less brick**: one sill per page (the footer's); the cream brick wall, the sky and navy tiles behind text and
  the This Sunday card's tile are gone (the card is plain navy with one 12px brick course on top); at most two
  brick surfaces in view. **Three surfaces**: the ground, white, and one tint `#E9EEF5`.
- **Markers only beside words**: the brick marker leads a worded eyebrow and nothing else; the section break
  is whitespace, and the hairline under the header is gone. The big brick cross is About's alone.
- **Page heads**: shorter (about 350px on desktop), H1 max 88px (64px for a sermon or a post); the page photo
  in the gable on the right, or the page's icon large at the right edge, centred on the title. Icons match
  their page and none repeats across the heads.
- **One H2 colour** (navy), one eyebrow, one button radius, one inline link, one step connector (2px dashed
  light navy), and the gable as the one photo shape (the stepped card corner is retired). Young Serif from
  24px up only. Full list: `docs/agent/design-tokens.md`, shapes: `docs/agent/shapes.md`.

North star: a small church that looks like itself. The stone mark is the most distinctive thing
the church owns, so the site is a calm, mostly one-colour frame for it. Clear Sunday facts first,
generous white space, honest about size. Must not look like the First Baptist Church Muncie site
(cream paper and ink, titling caps, indigo and gold bands).

## Colors

Decision 2026-10-21, applied the same day: the brand is **two families on a warm ground**.
Auburn (`#9A3B2A`, dark `#7E2E20`, light `#B5503A`; the colour the church is named after and
the colour of its brick) is the mark, the subject of each stone icon and at most one call to
action per page. Navy (`#1F3A5F`, dark `#16304D`, mid `#2C4F7C`, light `#7F98B8`; the
lettering on the church's own sign) is text, links, buttons, H2s, the Feature band and the
supporting pieces of each icon. Ink for body text is a deeper navy, `#16263D`. The ground is a
warm white, `#FBF8F3`, deliberately lighter than the `#F4EFE7` soft band (`--muted`) and far from
the FBC Muncie cream paper this brief rules out below; borders are `#E6DED2`. The Stone look is
the light navy tinted (`#E9EEF5`, gaps `#D3DCE8`). Cards stay pure white. Masters: `brand/logo/`
(the linocut brick-cross mark and lockups, wired in 2026-10-23) and `brand/icons/` (the stone icons, all drawn in brick courses since round 25 on 2026-10-24,
printed into `src/assets/brand/`). The old seven colours are gone from the CSS, the textures
(`public/textures/tile-*.svg`, file names kept), the Studio banner and the OG card.

- Roles: **navy** is the everyday colour (links, buttons, H2s). **Auburn** is the cross and at
  most one call to action per page. **Light auburn** and **light navy** are accents only (stepping
  stones, the mosaic, lettering on a navy band), never text on the ground. **Navy mid** is the
  second structural colour. **Ink** is body text.
- Measured contrast on the ground `#FBF8F3`: ink 14.4, navy 10.8, navy mid 7.9, auburn dark 8.6,
  auburn 6.5, muted text `#4A5A73` 6.6, light auburn 4.7 (large text and icons only), light navy
  2.8 (never text). White on navy 11.5, white on auburn 6.9. Ground on the ink band 15.2. Ink on
  the Stone band 11.0, navy on it 8.3. On the navy band: Feature captions `#E9EEF5` 9.9, light
  navy 3.9 (accent and large only), light auburn 2.3 (decoration only, never text). No text pair
  fell under 4.5:1; the two Studio lettering colours that would not read at 3:1 on the Studio
  tile were lifted (`#B5503A` to `#D98A74`).
- Light only (decided 2026-10-17): the site has no dark mode. The dark values in `brand.config.json` (primary lifted to `#7F98B8`) are kept as
  the palette for a dark band or a printed piece on ink, not a site theme.

## Typography

- **Young Serif** for display and H3 and below where a serif heading is wanted; one weight (400).
- **Familjen Grotesk** for body, UI, labels and the tracked caps of the place line ("FIRST BAPTIST
  CHURCH OF").
- **Brick finish** (2026-10-23): every brick on the site is printed like the logo, the linocut
  print traced from Gemini. Each brick has chipped edges and an uneven darker rim, heavier along the
  bottom, and white flecks and scratches show the ground through it (a mask of one repeating fleck
  pattern per drawing; painted pale flecks on the navy and sky tiles, where a hole would show the
  darker grout; a fleck texture on the CSS bricks). One module draws it:
  `scripts/lib/brick-print.mjs`, used by the path, sill, tiles, markers, share cards, Studio
  strips and the letterforms.
- **The building sketch** (2026-10-23, redrawn from the real site 2026-10-24, the building only): a pen-and-pencil rendering of the church, lifted off its
  paper and painted in navy (pale navy in the footer) through `BuildingSketch.astro`, as two masks
  (pen lines, pencil shading) so it draws itself in on scroll, lines first (2026-10-24). Used as the
  About "Our building" band and as the Plan your visit walk's opener with the
  front door pinned. Master and notes: `brand/illustration/`.
- **Brick letterforms: retired 2026-10-24.** Nathan dropped them everywhere as too busy: the page H1s and the 404 headline are plain navy Young Serif through `StoneHeading.astro`, the share cards set the title in cream Young Serif on the navy stone-tile ground (the "night" card, picked the same day, `docs/design-directions/2026-10-24-og-cards/`), and the sermon and series art with no picture sets its word in plain cream type. The generator and its tests stay in `scripts/lib/` unused, in case a single moment wants it back; what follows is the history. Decided 2026-10-21 for the big headings ( sheet 13 in
  `docs/design-directions/2026-10-21-brick-path/`; they replaced "Auburn Stone", Young Serif split into
  pebbles). Treatment A, brick-filled letters: each letter is a clean Young Serif glyph filled with a
  running-bond field in navy tones (the pale navy set on the dark footer), joints a hairline that
  wanders a little, a darker rim along each brick's bottom edge, the linocut flecks punched through
  (see "Brick finish" below), and every fourth letter (the "b" of Auburn) in auburn
  tones. It sets the page H1 and the Home masthead "Auburn". Treatment B, cut
  out of a wall: the word as negative space in a block of auburn brick whose course ends step in
  and out like the mark; the 404 headline and the sermon and series art with no picture. Both are
  drawn at build time by `scripts/lib/brick-letters.mjs` (outlines baked from Young Serif by
  `npm run gen:letters`) as one inline SVG per word, so a long title still wraps; the course height
  is a fixed fraction of the type size, so the brick reads at 96 px and at 420 px. The words stay
  real text (aria-label, a hidden copy and the preview's stega run). An H2 in one colour is plain
  Young Serif. The Studio banner lettering still uses the old Auburn Stone font until the Studio
  kit gets the generator.
- Body 18 px minimum, line length 60 to 72 characters.

24 px, row titles 20 px, times and dates 28 to 36 px; corners 4 / 10 / 14 / 20 px and round pills; card padding 20 to
28 px, cards 16 to 24 px apart.

## Components

- Logo (2026-10-23): the linocut print, a brick wall with a white cross cut out of it and enclosed
  in brick on every side, traced as Gemini printed it (grain, flecks and the darker rim kept). The
  full print shows at about 64 px and up (Home hero, page head, closing band), the small cut with no
  flecks below that (header, favicon, share cards, Studio). The cross is enclosed, so it reads on
  any light ground with no panel; on a dark ground the mark sits on its paper plate (the footer).
  A roadside-red version, with the cross filled `#D9262E`, is kept ready in
  `brand/logo/alternates/roadside-red/` in case the church prefers it, for large uses only.
  Horizontal lockup in the header; Auburn-led lockup where the place name should lead.
- Stone icons (2026-10-24, round 25: every icon is drawn in brick courses, like the Ministries set, so the older pebble
  mosaic is gone; 2026-10-23: printed like the brick, rugged and lived in: chipped edges, a darker rim in each tile's colour, grain and white flecks, baked by `scripts/print-icons.mjs` from the masters in `brand/icons/`) for the Sunday facts and the main entry points (clock, map pin, coffee, Bible,
  praying hands, giving hand); one size per page, never mixed with line icons.
- Buttons: one family, 10 px radius: auburn solid for the page's one main action, navy solid, and the navy
  outline. Pills only for chips and tags.
- Sunday block: times, address, parking and "what to expect" in one component, reused on Home and
  Plan Your Visit.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep one stone moment per page: the full-colour mark large once (About), the gable photo on its brick
  slab elsewhere.
- Do keep one red button per page and at most two brick surfaces in any one view.
- Do make every layout work with no photographs, and better with one.
- Do mark unconfirmed facts (service times, the verse) until the church confirms them.
- Don't put mosaic colour on H2s, borders, dividers or backgrounds.
- Don't use light auburn or light navy as text on the ground.
- Don't use stock photography, crowd shots or anything that makes the church look bigger than it is.
- Don't reuse the Muncie site's paper-and-ink ground, titling masthead or indigo bands.
