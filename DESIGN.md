## Overview

Status 2026-10-17: direction picked, A blended with B and C (`docs/design-directions/2026-10-17-fbc-auburn/`,
`## Decision`). Archetype: image-led, but airy and white, with the stone art carrying the page until
the photo session. Home opens with the masthead ("First Baptist Church of" over a huge brick-letter "Auburn") beside an arched photo; inner pages lead with a big stone icon and a brick-letter H1
beside an arched photo. Density airy. Imagery: arched photos (`ArchHero.astro`, the
text-and-picture band); with no photo yet the arch holds the brick-cross mark or a soft navy tint, never
a grey box. In the code: `src/styles/auburn.css` (the brand layer), `StoneHeading.astro`,
`ArchHero.astro`; the three stepping stones are the section divider on inner pages.

Update 2026-10-21, the brick brand: the pebble art is gone from the site. The section marker is
three stepped bricks from the mark's corner; the hero path is a low brick wall of three courses
stepping up to the This Sunday card; the card, the sermon art and the quiet panels sit on
running-bond tiles; the walk's numbered stones are numbered bricks. Generated by
`npm run gen:stones` from `scripts/lib/brick-courses.mjs` (decision:
`docs/design-directions/2026-10-21-brick-path/`). The letterforms followed the same evening
(see "Brick letterforms" under Typography): the masthead "Auburn", the footer signature and the page H1s
are brick-filled letters, and the 404 headline and the sermon art with no picture are cut out of a wall.

Update 2026-10-17, the bold home pass: Home carries a full-height ribbon of stones in the four
mark colours (`StoneRibbon.astro`, `src/scripts/ribbon.ts`) on a plain white page with no tinted
bands or dividers, a large red intro line in the hero, and photos (stock stand-ins until the photo
session). Decision: `docs/design-directions/2026-10-17-fbc-auburn/README.md`.

North star: a small church that looks like itself. The stone mark is the most distinctive thing
the church owns, so the site is a calm, mostly one-colour frame for it. Clear Sunday facts first,
generous white space, honest about size. Must not look like the First Baptist Church Muncie site
(cream paper and ink, titling caps, indigo and gold bands).

## Colors

Decision 2026-10-21, applied the same day: the brand is **two families on a warm ground**.
Auburn (`#9A3B2A`, dark `#7E2E20`, light `#B5503A`; the colour the church is named after and
the colour of its brick) is the mark, the subject of each stone icon and at most one call to
action per page. Navy (`#1F3A5F`, dark `#16304D`, mid `#2C4F7C`, light `#7F98B8`; the
lettering on the church's own sign) is text, links, buttons, H2s, the Feature band and the
supporting pieces of each icon. Ink for body text is a deeper navy, `#16263D`. The ground is a
warm white, `#FBF8F3`, deliberately lighter than the `#F4EFE7` soft band (`--muted`) and far from
the FBC Muncie cream paper this brief rules out below; borders are `#E6DED2`. The Stone look is
the light navy tinted (`#E9EEF5`, gaps `#D3DCE8`). Cards stay pure white. Masters: `brand/logo/`
(the brick cross mark and lockups, wired in 2026-10-21) and `brand/icons/` (the sixteen stone icons, now the
ones in `src/assets/brand/`). The old seven colours are gone from the CSS, the textures
(`public/textures/tile-*.svg`, file names kept), the Studio banner and the OG card.

- Roles: **navy** is the everyday colour (links, buttons, H2s). **Auburn** is the cross and at
  most one call to action per page. **Light auburn** and **light navy** are accents only (stepping
  stones, the mosaic, lettering on a navy band), never text on the ground. **Navy mid** is the
  second structural colour. **Ink** is body text.
- Measured contrast on the ground `#FBF8F3`: ink 14.4, navy 10.8, navy mid 7.9, auburn dark 8.6,
  auburn 6.5, muted text `#4A5A73` 6.6, light auburn 4.7 (large text and icons only), light navy
  2.8 (never text). White on navy 11.5, white on auburn 6.9. Ground on the ink band 15.2. Ink on
  the Stone band 11.0, navy on it 8.3. On the navy band: Feature captions `#E9EEF5` 9.9, light
  navy 3.9 (accent and large only), light auburn 2.3 (decoration only, never text). No text pair
  fell under 4.5:1; the two Studio lettering colours that would not read at 3:1 on the Studio
  tile were lifted (`#B5503A` to `#D98A74`).
- Light only (decided 2026-10-17): the site has no dark mode. The dark values in `brand.config.json` (primary lifted to `#7F98B8`) are kept as
  the palette for a dark band or a printed piece on ink, not a site theme.

## Typography

- **Young Serif** for display and H3 and below where a serif heading is wanted; one weight (400).
- **Familjen Grotesk** for body, UI, labels and the tracked caps of the place line ("FIRST BAPTIST
  CHURCH OF").
- **Brick letterforms** for the big headings, 40 px and up (decided 2026-10-21, sheet 13 in
  `docs/design-directions/2026-10-21-brick-path/`; they replaced "Auburn Stone", Young Serif split into
  pebbles). Treatment A, brick-filled letters: each letter is a clean Young Serif glyph filled with a
  running-bond field in navy tones (the pale navy set on the dark footer), joints a hairline, a
  darker band along each brick's bottom edge, and every fourth letter (the "b" of Auburn) in auburn
  tones. It sets the page H1, the Home masthead "Auburn" and the footer signature. Treatment B, cut
  out of a wall: the word as negative space in a block of auburn brick whose course ends step in
  and out like the mark; the 404 headline and the sermon and series art with no picture. Both are
  drawn at build time by `scripts/lib/brick-letters.mjs` (outlines baked from Young Serif by
  `npm run gen:letters`) as one inline SVG per word, so a long title still wraps; the course height
  is a fixed fraction of the type size, so the brick reads at 96 px and at 420 px. The words stay
  real text (aria-label, a hidden copy and the preview's stega run). An H2 in one colour is plain
  Young Serif. The Studio banner lettering still uses the old Auburn Stone font until the Studio
  kit gets the generator.
- Body 18 px minimum, line length 60 to 72 characters.

## Elevation

Flat. Sections separate by ground tint and space, not shadows. One soft shadow at most for a
floating element (a sticky "Plan your visit" bar on mobile).

## Components

- Logo: the brick cross (seven auburn courses, the cross cut out), horizontal lockup in the header (the five-course favicon under 24 px), Auburn-led lockup where the place
  name should lead (footer, a sign-like hero).
- Stone icons for the Sunday facts and the main entry points (clock, map pin, coffee, Bible,
  praying hands, giving hand); one size per page, never mixed with line icons.
- Buttons: one family, 10 px radius, deep sky solid and deep sky outline; red only for the single
  most important action on a page.
- Sunday block: times, address, parking and "what to expect" in one component, reused on Home and
  Plan Your Visit.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep one stone moment per page: the mosaic H1 or the full-colour mark, not both large.
- Do make every layout work with no photographs, and better with one.
- Do mark unconfirmed facts (service times, the verse) until the church confirms them.
- Don't put mosaic colour on H2s, borders, dividers or backgrounds.
- Don't draw brick letters below 40 px; the brick needs height to read.
- Don't use light auburn or light navy as text on the ground.
- Don't use stock photography, crowd shots or anything that makes the church look bigger than it is.
- Don't reuse the Muncie site's paper-and-ink ground, titling masthead or indigo bands.
